# River facts and flow references

The complete readable source/mouth inventory is generated in [My atlas](my-atlas.md#river-sources-and-mouths). The facts and downstream reference coordinates are curated in `scripts/process-countries.mjs`. A source can mean the start of the named river, a conventional source, or stated headwaters; it is not necessarily the most distant headwater of the entire drainage basin.

## References for expanded coverage

- Yukon: [Yukon River water isotope study](https://emrlibrary.gov.yk.ca/emrlibrary/ebooks/Northern%20Research%20Institute/yukon_river_water_isotope_data_2015.pdf) distinguishes the Marsh Lake outlet from the more distant glacial headwaters; [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/yukon-delta-alaska-72762/) describes the Bering Sea delta.
- Mackenzie and Athabasca: [Canada's Inland Waters Branch](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2018/eccc/En36-515-60-eng.pdf) describes Great Slave Lake, Lake Athabasca, and drainage to the Arctic; [Mackenzie basin review](https://iaac-aeic.gc.ca/050/documents/48587/48587E.pdf) identifies the Beaufort Sea.
- Ohio: [US National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/articles/the-ohio-river.htm).
- Arkansas: [Encyclopedia of Arkansas](https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/Arkansas-River-2225/).
- Tennessee: [Legacy Parks Foundation](https://legacyparks.org/places/french-broad-river/) describes the French Broad–Holston confluence; [Tennessee River overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee_River) identifies the Ohio confluence near Paducah.
- Potomac: [West Virginia State Parks](https://wvstateparks.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/WVSP-Digital-Guide.pdf) documents the North Branch spring at Fairfax Stone; [Potomac overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potomac_River) describes Chesapeake Bay.
- Rio Grande: [US National Park Service](https://www.nps.gov/articles/southwest-river-environments.htm) and [US Fish and Wildlife Service](https://www.fws.gov/rivers/river/rio-grande-new-mexico).
- St. Lawrence: [Canadian Hydrographic Service](https://tides.gc.ca/en/great-lakes-st-lawrence-river-system) and [St. Lawrence Action Plan](https://www.planstlaurent.qc.ca/en/st-lawrence-river).
- Senegal: [FAO](https://www.fao.org/4/d0780b/D0780B04.htm) and [Senegal basin authority](https://soged-omvs.org/le-fleuve-senegal/).
- Volta: [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/volta_res-b044a9af-083a-11e0-9962-d5ce3506d72e/) and [Store norske leksikon](https://snl.no/Volta).
- White Nile: [Sudan water resources review](https://internationalwaterlaw.org/bibliography/articles/Salman/SudanWaterResources.pdf) distinguishes the named White Nile beginning at Lake No from its upstream lake-fed system.
- Blue Nile: [World Bank environmental assessment](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/879081468005721207/pdf/E22310v10P11671meworks010Procedures.pdf); [NASA on the Khartoum confluence](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/two-niles-meet-81186/).
- Orange: [ORASECOM basin assessment](https://orasecom.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/Orange-Senqu-River-Basin-Preliminary-Transboundary-Diagnostic-Analysis.pdf) and [South African History Online](https://sahistory.org.za/place/orange-river).
- Limpopo: [Limpopo Watercourse Commission](https://limpopocommission.org/the-basin/the-river-basin/hydrology/hydrology-of-the-limpopo-river-basin/surface-water/).
- Loire and Rhône: [French Ministry of the Interior geography atlas](https://formation-civique.interieur.gouv.fr/fiches-par-thematiques/histoire-geographie-et-culture/atlas-de-la-france/les-fleuves-fran%C3%A7ais/); [Larousse on the Rhône](https://www.larousse.fr/encyclopedie/riviere-lac/Rh%C3%B4ne/140848).
- Po and Tiber: [historical Po description](https://jimclifford.ca/britannica-articles-4/a/EB.15/193469092/58.html) and [Britannica Tiber description reproduced by The Latin Library](https://www.thelatinlibrary.com/historians/notes/tiber.html).
- Thames: [Thames Rivers Trust](https://www.thamesriverstrust.org.uk/the-thames-river-basin/). Thames Head is identified as the conventional source.
- Dnieper: [Encyclopedia of Ukraine](https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pagesD%5CN%5CDniproRiver); [Dnieper overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dnieper).
- Ural: [Ural river overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ural_%28river%29).
- Songhua: [NASA](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/songhua-river-6139/).
- Yalu: [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://english.igsnrr.cas.cn/ecg/naturalscenery/rivers/202011/t20201119_251612.html).
- Pearl: [WWF Pearl River Delta report](https://awsassets.wwfhk.panda.org/downloads/prd_report_2007.pdf) and [World Bank hydrology assessment](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/908481468746347586/pdf/E8290vol1030revised0paper.pdf). - Tarim: [China encyclopedia entry](https://www.berkshirepublishing.com/ecph-china/2018/01/13/tarim-river/) describes its contributing headwaters; [2024 Taitema Lake study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e29575) describes regulated ecological water delivery to the terminal lake. - Irrawaddy: [Himalayan Club source investigation](https://www.himalayanclub.org/hj/60/17/source-of-the-irrawaddy-and-gorge-country/); [USGS delta imagery](https://eros.usgs.gov/earthshots/ayeyarwady-delta-myanmar).
- Salween: [International Rivers](https://www.internationalrivers.org/where-we-work/asia/salween/).
- Narmada: [National River Conservation Directorate report](https://v1.wii.gov.in/images/images/documents/ganga_project/NRCD_Narmada_Report.pdf).
- Godavari: [Maharashtra water regulator study](https://mwrra.maharashtra.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Godavari-Study-Group-Report.pdf).
- Krishna: [Krishna River Management Board](https://krmb.gov.in/krmb/aboutUs).

## Animation checks

The animation depicts downstream travel along stored centerlines. All selected rivers have an explicit approximate downstream reference, including tributary confluences and inland sinks. Name-changing reaches are pooled into one network. Distinct neighbours determine topology despite duplicate digitizations; weighted network distances orient parts toward downstream endpoints. Multiple coastal delta outlets are treated as sinks, rather than routing one distributary back inland toward another.

Run `node --test scripts/check-rivers.test.mjs` after regeneration. Checks cover Danube/Donau continuity, duplicate linework, disconnected components, delta outlets, complete cards, and a downstream audit of the generated selection. This checks the curated centerline model, not instantaneous hydrology, tides, or seasonal discharge.
