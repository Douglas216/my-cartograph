## Cross-reference: which peaks feed which range's color

53 of the 76 peaks above fall geometrically inside one of the 129 range polygons and
were used as the `max_elev` source for that range's fill darkness (decision 10 /
`RANGE_MAX_ELEV` in `process-countries.mjs`). The other 23 don't fall inside any range
polygon and only appear as standalone `▲` peak labels: Mount Kilimanjaro, Mount Takahe,
Mount Sidley, Gongga Shan, Nganglong Kangri, Geladandong, Mount Kenya, Klyuchevskaya
Sopka, Gora Manas, Kuh-e Jang Qal'eh, Gunung Kinabalu, Cerro Champaquí, Kinyeti, Mount
Elgon, Lesatima, Gora Korolenko, Magen Gangri, Daba Shan, Mazong Shan,
Gora Burun-Shibertuy, Inyaptuk Golets, Wheeler Peak, Sacajawea Peak.
