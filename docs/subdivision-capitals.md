# Selected first-level subdivision capitals

Russia, mainland China, India, USA, Brazil, Canada and Mexico retain first-level capitals regardless of source population. `FIRST_LEVEL_CAPITALS` in the pipeline maps source city names to the units they serve; source feature IDs prevent namesakes (e.g. Richmond, Montpelier, Krasnogorsk) from being promoted accidentally. Historical or erroneous Natural Earth capital flags are excluded. Moscow and Beijing remain national capitals, never duplicate dots or table roles. Direct-administered cities and federal districts are included. Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan keep the atlas's existing territorial treatment.

Canada has 13 provincial/territorial capitals plus Ottawa; USA has 50 state capitals plus Washington; Brazil has 26 state capitals plus Brasília; Mexico has 31 state capitals plus Mexico City. Russia follows internationally recognized borders: 83 federal subjects, excluding claimed Ukrainian territory. Gatchina serves Leningrad Oblast; Magas replaces Nazran. Krasnogorsk is included as the Moscow Oblast government seat, with an explicit note that the oblast has no official capital. Supplemental points have no invented population figures (the catalog shows a dash).

India includes shared Chandigarh, seasonal/second capitals Jammu, Dharamshala, Gairsain and Nagpur, and joint Ladakh seats Leh/Kargil. Daman serves the merged Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu territory; Silvassa's obsolete first-level flag is removed. Seasonal qualifiers do not create different role names.

The map keeps red dots for subdivision capitals and larger red stars for national capitals. The selected first-level capitals have an independent toggle; below-one-million seats appear at z5, alongside subdivision borders, while million-plus seats retain z3 visibility. The Cities catalog uses National capital / First-level subdivision capital / City, with Capital of plus role and country filters.
