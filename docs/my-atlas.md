# My geographical atlas

A growing record of the places and features I have come to know. No tiers.

Generated by `node scripts/process-countries.mjs`; edit PERSONAL_* in that script to change membership. Raw geometry remains a reusable library. Themes, zoom and collisions still determine label visibility.

All countries/territories remain. All five oceans remain; named marine areas follow PERSONAL_MARINE_EXCLUDED. Cities: national capitals plus population >= 1 million in the source dataset. Subdivision selections use Natural Earth continents (Russia is Europe); territories follow their geographical continent. The listed European countries are included throughout their available subdivision geometry. Sierra Nevada means the US range. Caspian and Black Sea remain marine features. Aral Sea is included as North and South Aral Sea geometry from the source dataset, not a reconstructed historical shoreline. River labels use the familiar identity at overview zoom and local reach names with that identity from zoom 6.5; selecting any reach highlights the whole river.
